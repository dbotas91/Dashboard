## Fix your Wi-Fi connection

### If streaming constantly buffers

    ![An Amazon block next to a TV with an ethernet cable inside](https://static0.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/an-amazon-block-next-to-a-tv-with-an-ethernet-cable-inside.jpg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)Credit: Amazon

First, check whether the problem is actually with the network by seeing if your other devices are working okay.

Next, make sure the Fire Stick is getting a good connection to your router. As with the heat issue, keeping it stuck away behind the TV can disrupt the signal and mean it doesn't get as strong a connection as you need, especially when streaming high-bitrate video, like 4K movies. Try to move either the stick or the router so they're closer to each other.

Also, consider switching between the 5GHz and 2.4GHz networks, depending on how far apart the devices are. If they're near each other, 5GHz can be the better option. If none of that works and you still suspect Wi-Fi is the cause, [consider using an Ethernet adapter instead](https://www.howtogeek.com/my-fire-tv-stick-kept-buffering-until-i-got-this-essential-accessory/). This will give you a much more reliable internet connection.

Fire Sticks will often slow down and start crashing [when they run short on storage space](https://www.howtogeek.com/fire-tv-stick-slow-down-for-one-stupid-reason-and-it-takes-30-seconds-to-fix/).
